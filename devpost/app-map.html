DataTrail — Architecture & Code Tour

Guided map tracing how personal data flows from raw policy clauses into the interactive 4-stage trail.

1 Ingestion & Real Policy Samples

src/data/samples.ts & src/components/PolicyInputBar.tsx

Hosts real-world Indian delivery, fintech, and social messaging privacy policies (Swiggy, Zomato, WhatsApp). Provides instant tabs for real samples, raw clipboard paste with live word counting, plain text file upload, and URL fetching with honest error boundaries.

2 Deterministic Policy Analyzer Engine

src/engine/analyzer.ts & src/engine/patterns.ts

Tokenizes legal documents into normalized clauses. Scans for personal data categories (Geolocation, Device IDs, Contacts, Financial, Communications, Biometrics). Classifies evidence into the 4 stages: COLLECT, USE, SHARE, and KEEP.

3 Zero-Hallucination Quote Verifier

src/engine/quoteVerifier.ts

Mechanically guarantees that every extracted sentence exists verbatim in the source text using substring indexing. Identifies and flags corporate legal hedges (e.g. "including but not limited to", "as long as necessary", "commercial partners").

4 Interactive 4-Stage Trail Board

src/components/TrailBoard.tsx & src/components/TrailNodeCard.tsx

Renders the central COLLECT → USE → SHARE → KEEP pathway with animated SVG energy conduits and color-coded honesty badges (Stated, Vague, Not Found).

5 Evidence Drawer & Necessity Analysis

src/components/EvidenceDrawer.tsx & src/components/NecessityCard.tsx

Provides deep inspection with verbatim sentence quotes, paragraph context, and line numbers. Separates factual policy quotes from analytical necessity assessments, maintaining strict transparency regarding runtime uncertainty.

Core Developer Takeaway: DataTrail demonstrates that privacy intelligence is most effective when it avoids black-box scores and generative summaries, delivering verifiable verbatim evidence in an interactive structural map.
